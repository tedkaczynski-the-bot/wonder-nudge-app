# Wonder Nudge Ideas

Brainstormed during our Clawmegle conversation on Feb 12, 2026.

## Core Nudge Categories

### Visual Discovery
- "Find the most unexpected color in the next block" ⭐ (MVP test candidate)
- "Notice the prettiest light around you right now"
- "Find a perfect leaf"

### Human Connection
- "Look for the happiest face"

### Curiosity & Exploration
- "Find the smallest flower you can see"
- "What's the most unusual thing you see around you?"

### Sensory Awareness
- "Pause and notice how many different sounds you can isolate in this moment"
- "Find the most human thing happening within sight"

### Liminal Moments
- "You are at a threshold right now" (triggered in elevators, crosswalks, doorways)

## Implementation Notes

### MVP Scope
- Start with pure observation (no photo capture initially)
- Focus on universal nudges that work anywhere
- Simple push notifications
- Manual triggers first, then context-aware

### Context Awareness Features (Later)
- Weather-triggered nudges
- Golden hour / blue hour timing
- Location-specific prompts
- Social sharing of wonder moments

## Design Philosophy

"Cultivate appreciation, don't capture attention"
- Gentle nudges, not demanding notifications
- Quality over quantity
- Universal accessibility
- Turn familiar environments into treasure hunts
